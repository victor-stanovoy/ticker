    05 · Keep and share

    Take it with you.

    Your setup is saved on this device. Copy a link to open the same setup anywhere. Your Google client ID is never included.

    Good to know

    Keep it open

    The tab has to stay open for the title to move. Pin it so it takes little space and always shows its title start.

    Why a ticker?

    Browsers cut long titles off. Scrolling a short window lets a full headline pass through the tab.

    Background tabs

    Browsers slow timers in hidden tabs. A tiny worker keeps the beat, but some browsers will still step more slowly.

    About email

    A web page can only read your mail with your permission. The Inbox source uses your own Google client ID and a read-only scope, and nothing leaves your browser.
